Fill the three playlists

Music plays the whole time. By day, one playlist covers everything: wandering round town and fishing. At night it splits, so night fishing gets its own sound. When night falls, day breaks, or you start or stop fishing after dark, the music fades smoothly into the next playlist. Drop a song file onto a playlist to fill its next slot. Everyone with the team link sees the same board as it changes, and you can listen back to or download anything that's in.

    Before you export

    • Send MP3 or M4A if you can. WAV, OGG, FLAC and Opus also work, but they take more of the shared storage.
    • The game plays music in mono at 24 kHz. Wide stereo effects won't come through, so check your mix in mono.
    • Loudness is evened out automatically, and silence at the start is trimmed, so you don't need to match levels between songs.
    • Songs in a playlist play back to back with no gap. Endings that lead nicely into another song work best.
    • When night falls or day breaks, and when you start or stop fishing at night, the music fades over a few seconds into the other playlist, often partway through a song. The two night playlists blend best when they sit at a similar tempo, even if they feel different.
    • A day lasts about 20 real minutes and a night about 7. Night songs come round less often, so fewer are needed.
