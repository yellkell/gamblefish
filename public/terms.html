Terms of purchase

These terms cover buying coins at the Island Bank in Fish & Chips.

Who can buy

You must be 18 or over to buy coins.

What coins are

Coins are a virtual currency for playing Fish & Chips, a game played for fun. They have no cash value. They can never be withdrawn, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, goods, or prizes of any kind, and nothing won with them is worth money.

Paying

Payments are taken by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Your coins are added to your game once Stripe confirms the payment, usually within seconds.

Keeping your coins

Save your purchases to your email at the Island Bank. You can then log in on another headset to get your game back, including coins you've bought and haven't spent yet. Coins you have spent or lost in play are not restored.

Refunds

Coins are delivered straight away, so a purchase can't be cancelled once they have arrived, except where the law says otherwise. If something went wrong with a payment, contact us and we'll put it right.
