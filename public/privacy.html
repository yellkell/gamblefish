Privacy

Gamble Fish gives each headset an anonymous account (Firebase Authentication), so your game can be saved. We keep your game save (your wallet, backpack, catch log and upgrades) and a record of the coins you have bought.

If you save your purchases to an email, we store that email with your account so you can log in on another headset. We use it only to send you sign-in links.

Payments are handled by Stripe, which receives the details you type at checkout. We receive only the amount, what was bought, and the email you gave.

We don't sell or share your data. To have your account and data deleted, email yellkellbusiness@gmail.com.
